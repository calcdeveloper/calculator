## Common Mistakes Users Make

Even though the tool provides absolute formatting perfection, the human element of *deploying* the dummy text can ruin a professional presentation. Avoid these massive traps:

1.  **Forgetting to Remove It Before Launch:** This is the ultimate nightmare for every web developer. A designer uses Lorem Ipsum to scaffold the homepage, the team forgets to replace it with the real marketing copy, and the website goes live to the public featuring a massive block of Latin gibberish. Always run a final "Find and Replace" scan on your codebase before launching!
2.  **Using It for Microcopy:** Lorem Ipsum is brilliant for massive paragraphs. However, it is terrible for "microcopy" (tiny text like the words on a Submit Button, a navigation menu, or an error message). These elements rely entirely on context. For buttons and menus, you should always write real, functional text (like "Buy Now" or "Home") instead of using Latin.
3.  **Ignoring Real Content Lengths:** If you know your client's products only have very short, 2-sentence descriptions, do not design a massive UI card that requires 4 paragraphs of Lorem Ipsum to look balanced. When the real, tiny text is finally injected, the design will look incredibly empty and broken. Always generate placeholder text that matches the expected length of the final real content!
