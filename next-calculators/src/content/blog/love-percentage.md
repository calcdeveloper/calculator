## How does the Calculator work? (The Algorithm)

Because the calculator is designed for entertainment rather than scientific peer review, it does not actually analyze your psychological attachment style. However, the software does use genuine mathematical algorithms to ensure the results are consistent. If you type the exact same two names tomorrow, you will get the exact same result.

Here is a look at the common digital algorithms used behind the scenes to calculate your romantic fate:

### 1. The "TRUE LOVE" Letter Matching Method
This is the oldest and most traditional algorithm, adapted from the pen-and-paper games of the 1990s. The algorithm takes both names and counts how many times the letters from the words **T-R-U-E** and **L-O-V-E** appear in them.
*   *Example:* If the names are "Romeo" and "Juliet", the algorithm counts how many R's, O's, E's, L's, etc., are present.
*   It takes those counts, lines them up as a string of numbers, and mathematically adds adjacent numbers together until it shrinks down to a two-digit percentage. 

### 2. The ASCII Character Value Method
Every letter on a computer keyboard has a hidden, underlying numerical value (known as an ASCII code). For example, a capital 'A' might be 65, and 'B' is 66.
*   The calculator breaks down both names into their hidden numerical codes.
*   It adds up all the numbers for Person A, and all the numbers for Person B.
*   It then runs these massive sums through a complex mathematical formula (often involving a modulo operation) to generate a pseudo-random, yet completely consistent, percentage between 1 and 100.

### 3. The Vowel-to-Consonant Ratio
Some modern algorithms analyze the phonetic structure of the names. They compare the number of vowels to the number of consonants in both names to find "harmony." If the names have a mathematically harmonious ratio, the algorithm boosts the Love Percentage closer to 100%.

Regardless of which specific mathematical engine is running in the background, the result is always instantaneous, consistent, and completely unique to the specific combination of names you entered.
