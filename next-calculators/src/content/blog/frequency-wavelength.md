4.  **Assuming High Frequency means Faster Velocity:** This is a conceptual trap. A high-frequency blue light does NOT travel faster than a low-frequency red light. ## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**1. What is the fundamental difference between Frequency and Wavelength?**
Frequency (Hertz) is a measurement of Time—how many complete wave cycles occur in exactly one second. Wavelength (Meters) is a measurement of physical Space—the actual length of the wave measured with a ruler from peak to peak.

**2. Are Light and Radio actually the same thing?**

The ONLY difference between the Wi-Fi going to your laptop and the red light coming from a laser pointer is the Frequency. (Light vibrates vastly faster than Wi-Fi).

**3. Why do large concert speakers only produce Bass?**

To efficiently physically push a wave of that massive size through the air, you need a massive physical speaker cone. **4. What is the Speed of Light in a vacuum?**
The exact physical constant (c) is **299,792,458 meters per second**. For standard engineering calculations, this is universally rounded to 300,000,000 m/s (or 3 x 10 to the power of 8).

**5. Why do dog whistles work?**

**6. What does "Redshift" mean in astronomy?**
Because of the Doppler Effect, if a galaxy is moving incredibly fast away from Earth, the light waves it emits get physically stretched out over the massive distance. This stretching increases the Wavelength. In the spectrum of visible light, a longer wavelength pushes the color toward Red. This mathematical shift proves the universe is expanding.

Use the calculator to find the exact Wavelength of your broadcast frequency. Standard radio antennas are typically cut to a "Quarter-Wave" (1/4 of the total wavelength) or a "Half-Wave" (1/2 of the total wavelength) to achieve perfect resonance.
