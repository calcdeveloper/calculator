## How does the Tool work? (The Algorithm)

It might seem like a simple parlor trick, but generating a mathematically pure, perfectly unbiased 50/50 split is actually a serious computational process. Physical coins are often flawed; the "Heads" side of a US quarter weighs slightly more than the "Tails" side, meaning a physical coin flip is actually closer to 51/49 in favor of the heavier side. 

How does computer code ensure absolute, 50.00% fairness? Here is a look at the fascinating computer science used behind the scenes:

### 1. The Core PRNG Engine
When you click the flip button, the tool activates a Pseudo-Random Number Generator (PRNG). The algorithm grabs a tiny, chaotic, constantly shifting variable from your computer's system (usually the exact current millisecond on the internal clock, like 842). 

### 2. The Modulo 2 Operation (Binary Logic)
Once the PRNG generates a massive, random internal number based on that millisecond seed, the algorithm applies a strict mathematical operation called "Modulo 2." This operation divides the random number by 2 and calculates the remainder. 
*   If the random internal number is even, the remainder is exactly 0 (which the software translates to **Tails**).
*   If the random internal number is odd, the remainder is exactly 1 (which the software translates to **Heads**).

### 3. Eliminating Human Physics
As mentioned, physical coin flips are flawed by weight distribution, wind resistance, and the specific thumb strength of the person flipping it. Some magicians can even practice flipping a coin to guarantee it lands on Heads every time. The digital PRNG has zero mass, zero gravity, and cannot be manipulated by human hands. It is the purest, most mathematically perfect coin toss you can possibly achieve.
