## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**1. What does MRP mean in India?**
MRP stands for Maximum Retail Price. It is a legally mandated system in India designed to protect consumers. The price printed on the box MUST include all local and federal taxes. A retailer is legally forbidden from adding GST on top of the MRP at the checkout counter. (However, they can offer discounts *below* the MRP).

**2. Why do American stores add tax at the register instead of the sticker?**
The United States has an incredibly fragmented tax system. Every single state, county, and even specific city has a completely different sales tax rate. It is impossible for a national corporation to print a single price tag that works everywhere, so they print the pre-tax base price and let the local computer calculate the local tax at the register.

**3. What is a "Trade Discount"?**
A trade discount is a B2B (Business to Business) pricing strategy. A manufacturer will sell a product to a retail store at a massive discount (e.g., 40% off the public catalog price) so the retail store has enough profit margin to sell it to the general public.

**4. How does "Cashback" differ from a Discount?**
A discount lowers the amount of money you pay at the register. "Cashback" (often offered by credit cards) forces you to pay the full price at the register, but returns a percentage of the money to your bank account weeks later. Mathematically, a discount is superior because it prevents your cash from being temporarily tied up.

**5. What is the difference between GST and VAT?**
GST (Goods and Services Tax) and VAT (Value Added Tax) are very similar indirect taxation systems used by governments globally. The primary difference is structural: VAT is collected at every single stage of production, while GST is usually a single comprehensive tax applied at the final point of consumption.

**6. Do I pay tax on the shipping fee?**
This depends entirely on the laws of your local government. In many jurisdictions, shipping is considered a taxable service, meaning the 10% tax will be applied to both the cost of the item AND the cost of the shipping.

**7. Why do luxury brands never offer percentage discounts?**
Luxury brands (like Rolex or Louis Vuitton) rely on the psychology of exclusivity. If they offer a "30% off clearance sale," it destroys the psychological illusion that their products are rare and highly valuable. They would rather destroy unsold inventory than cheapen their brand with percentage discounts.
