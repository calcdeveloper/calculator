Because the tool is built exclusively for entertainment and not for clinical psychological diagnosis, it does not use an MRI machine to scan the frontal lobe of your brain. However, the software does rely on a very clever, highly structured point-based weighting algorithm. 

Here is a look at the common digital logic used behind the scenes to calculate your mental maturity:

### 1. The Weighted Point System
Every single question in the quiz has a hidden mathematical value attached to its answers. 
*   If you select an answer that is highly associated with Gen Z (e.g., using current internet slang), the algorithm adds a very small number (like 15) to your hidden total.
*   If you select an answer associated with a mature adult (e.g., worrying about your credit score or complaining about loud music), the algorithm adds a large number (like 45) to your hidden total.

As you progress through the questions, the calculator is secretly keeping a running tally of your score. Once you click the final submit button, the algorithm adds up all your points and divides them by the total number of questions. This generates an "average" score that corresponds directly to a specific age bracket.

The algorithm is carefully designed by developers to target specific, universally recognized archetypes:
