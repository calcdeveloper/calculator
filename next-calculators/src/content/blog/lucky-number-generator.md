## How does the Tool work? (The Algorithm)

It might seem contradictory to use a highly logical computer to generate something as unscientific as "luck," but the underlying mechanics are actually deeply fascinating. How does computer code simulate destiny?

Here is a look at the fascinating computer science used behind the scenes to generate your lucky digits:

### 1. The Core PRNG Engine
At the absolute core of the tool is an algorithm known as a PRNG (Pseudo-Random Number Generator). Because computers are strictly rule-based machines, they cannot generate true "chaos." Instead, they must use a massive, constantly shifting variable to start a complex mathematical equation. This is usually the exact current millisecond on your device's internal clock (e.g., 914).

### 2. The Algorithmic Transformation
Once the tool captures that millisecond seed, it multiplies it by a massive prime number, adds another prime number, and then runs a "modulo" operation (finding the remainder after division) to chop the massive calculation down to fit perfectly within the boundaries you need (for example, between 1 and 99).

Because the computer clock is shifting thousands of times a second, it is physically impossible for a human to time their click to force a specific outcome.
